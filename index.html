Section 06

How We Use & Process Information

We process collected telemetry and technical logs under the following lawful bases:

  • Application Functionality & Performance: To maintain seamless runtime execution, save user preferences, and deliver core features.
  • Diagnostics & Crash Resolution: To analyze stack traces, memory leaks, and operating system incompatibilities to deliver software updates and patches.

Section 07

Data Retention & User Data Deletion Requests

We retain diagnostic logs and telemetry only for as long as necessary to address software stability (typically a rolling cycle of 30 to 90 days), after which logs are permanently deleted or irreversibly anonymized.

Data Deletion Procedure (App Store & Google Play Requirement)

You hold the right to request deletion of any telemetry, account data, or stored profile info associated with you.

  1. Submit an email request to support@yourdomain.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request - [App Name].
  2. Provide your app installation ID, registered email address, or advertising ID (accessible from your device settings).
  3. Our support team will process the request, purge all matching database entries, and confirm deletion within 30 calendar days.

Note: For account-free apps operating purely on-device, simply clearing the app cache/storage or uninstalling the app permanently purges all local data.

Section 09

Security Standards & Safeguards

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security controls to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These safeguards include:

  • Transport Layer Security: All client-server communications are encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS / HTTPS encryption.
  • Sandboxed Storage: On-device databases utilize encrypted file storage where applicable and operate inside operating system sandboxes.
  • Access Restrictions: Administrative access to analytics dashboards is strictly restricted through multi-factor authentication (MFA) and least-privilege principles.

While we strive to employ commercially acceptable means to protect information, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.
